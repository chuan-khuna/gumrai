กำไร กำไร

Soft to touch. Exact to the satang.

Gumrai is a bubblegum app for serious numbers. It reads like a sweet shop, so a seller enjoys opening it. Every baht in it is worked out to two decimals.

Gumrai is a pun: กำไร (profit) is said "gum-rai". The pink circle on กำ is a bubble. Press it.

Colour is a pack of gum flavours

Each flavour has one job, and the jobs never swap. Pink means action and brand. Mint means money left over. Blue raspberry means linked. Cinnamon means a loss. Grape is a single pop when something succeeds.

Two Thai faces, both round

Mitr has soft corners and loopless Thai letters, and it does the shouting. IBM Plex Sans Thai Looped does the reading. Its looped heads are the Thai most sellers learned at school, and its figures line up in tables.

Thai rules: line-height at least 1.35 on headings and 1.7 on body, or the tone marks clip. Letter-spacing stays at 0, because tracking pulls stacked vowels off their consonants. Thai has no capitals, so there are no uppercase labels and no italics.

Chewy, not glossy

Everything is round, and nothing is glass. A card is a soft slab: a 20px radius and two quiet shadows, one for contact and one for air. Only things you can press lift off the paper.

card · 20px

--shadow-card. Sections, sheets, the cost table.

lifted

--shadow-lift. A pressable card on hover, and the tilted sample.

flat tint

No shadow. Money cards and states sit on a tint instead.

Radii: card 20 · field 12 · chip and button 999. Dividers are dashed 1.5px hairlines in --color-line. Square corners don't exist here.

One pink push button per screen, for the main action. Mint push is for "keep this". Soft and outline are for everything else. Delete is a cinnamon outline, never a filled button.

Fields

Empty state & alert

ยังไม่มีชีตต้นทุน ตั้งชื่อสิ่งที่ขายเพื่อเริ่มคิดต้นทุนและกำไร

บันทึกไม่ได้ เพราะชื่อนี้มีในลิสต์ต้นทุนแล้ว

An empty state says what's missing, then gives the one next step. An alert says what happened and why, in one sentence.

Money is never decoration

The cheerful look stops at the numbers. Figures are exact, aligned and labelled, and they only ever come from the seller's own data.

  • Two decimals, always. Format with Intl.NumberFormat('th-TH', {minimumFractionDigits: 2}). Figures are stored as Postgres numeric, never as floats.
  • Tabular figures everywhere a number can sit above another number. The body font does this by default.
  • Right-align money and quantities in tables and inputs.
  • Name the Sale Unit next to every per-sale figure, for example กำไรต่อแก้ว, never just กำไร.
  • A loss gets three signals: the word ขาดทุน, a true minus sign (U+2212), and cinnamon.
  • The baht sign follows the number as a small suffix: 13.95 ฿, or 4 ฿/g for a Unit Cost.
  • One big figure per screen, the Net Profit, at --text-num. Everything else stays at body size.
  • Never invent a number. Empty data reads ยังไม่มี…, not a made-up example.

Motion: press, chew, pop

Plain CSS, no motion library. The app moves in three ways only. Buttons press down, the bubble chews slowly, and a save pops a grape star. Everything else just changes colour.

MomentMotionTokens
Button hover / presslift 2px, then down 3px with the edge shrinking. No scale.--ease-press · --dur-fast / --dur-press
Pressable card hoverlift 3–4px, shadow deepens--ease-spring · --dur-base
The bubblechews 1 → 1.08 every 4s. Hover inflates it, a click pops it and it grows back.--ease-spring · --dur-pop
Save succeedsgrape star-burst from the button, fires once--color-grape · --dur-pop
Field focus, chip, navcolour and ring only--ease-out · --dur-fast

With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, nothing moves position, the bubble holds still, and the star-burst is off. Colour changes stay, at 120ms or less. Focus rings show instantly and are never animated.

Never

  • Pink on a number. Bubblegum is for actions. Money is mint or cinnamon.
  • Gradients between flavours. Each flavour owns its own surface.
  • Pure white paper or pure black ink.
  • Square corners on cards, fields, chips or buttons.
  • Italic or uppercase, in Thai or English.
  • Tracking on Thai, or a heading line-height under 1.35.
  • More than one bubble or more than one pink push button per screen.
  • Emoji as icons, or celebration toasts. The star-burst is the celebration.
  • A colour outside globals.css. Components use the token utilities.
  • Glass, blur or neon glow. Gum is matte.
